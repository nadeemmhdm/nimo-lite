PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS
Your workspace boundaries
  • Local data and model files stay on this device.
  • Models have no shell or unrestricted network tools.
  • Dataset text and model output are always untrusted.
  • Training examples need explicit approval.
Storage is not encrypted. Protect your OS account and disk. Back up the data directory while the app is stopped. Deleting examples does not remove what checkpoints may have learned.
